Bring in last year and this year's figures

Drop files here or

You can drop several files at once. The tool recognises each one by its contents.

Last year

  • Last year's working papers (.xlsx with Expenses, Tax and CA sheets). Brings forward the Use of Home items and splits, the CA calculation and balances, and each Tax sheet line as a comparative.
  • Last year's accounts PDF (the Unaudited Financial Statements). Gives last year's Profit and Loss column and the client's address.
  • Last year's tax return (the HMRC SA100 PDF). Gives last year's income, reliefs, student loan, Child Benefit, tax and payments on account.
  • Last year's export from this tool (.xlsx). Rolls everything forward. If it's the same tax year, it reopens it for editing.

This year, from the Extraction Tool

  • Bank Statement or Bills CSV. Lines coded to the Use of Home account are added to this year's Use of Home costs.
  • Xero Account Transactions export (.csv). Reads the Use of Home account by name, plus any "1 of 3 rooms" adjustment.
  • Xero Profit and Loss export (.xlsx or .csv). Fills this year's Profit and Loss on the Accounts tab.
  • Extraction Tool backup (.json). Looks up this client's Use of Home account code.

    Export

    One Excel workbook with Summary, Use of Home, CA, Tax and Tax return sheets. The Summary sheet works out the tax with formulas, so you can type in a different trading profit there. Drop the workbook back in next year to roll everything forward, or this year to carry on editing.

    Audit file

    Everything about this client's year in one readable PDF, for the client file: progress, the files brought in and when, the meeting (notes, checklist, information received and adjustments agreed), the accounts line by line, Use of Home, capital allowances and tax workings with their notes, the tax return figures, the Xero journals, and the emails.

    Xero manual journals

    One CSV for Xero → Accounting → Manual journals → Import. Use of Home and capital allowances come in as two journals, each with its balancing figure to the clearing account.

    Tax rates

    The rates and allowances the tool uses for this tax year, for England, Wales and Northern Ireland. They are built in and checked against HMRC each year, so you only need this if HMRC changes something mid-year or a year isn't built in yet. Changes are saved in this browser and go into each export.
